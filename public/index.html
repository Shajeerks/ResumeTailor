In use for job search

Free option, recommended: choose JSearch to get real, live postings from LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, Glassdoor and company career sites (via Google for Jobs). Your AI key above then scores each posting against your resume. JSearch's free Basic plan gives 200 requests a month, and one search uses 1–4 requests.

How to get a free JSearch key (2 minutes)
  1. Go to rapidapi.com and sign up (Google sign-in works, no card needed).
  2. Search for JSearch (by OpenWeb Ninja) and open it.
  3. Open Pricing and subscribe to the Basic plan ($0).
  4. Open Endpoints, copy the X-RapidAPI-Key value and paste it below.

Or use a Gemini key whose project has billing turned on, or a Claude key. A Gemini app subscription (Gemini Pro) does not raise API limits.
